\section*{Highlights}

\begin{itemize}
\item Amyloid PET status was used as the primary AD biomarker label in OASIS-3.
\item The PET-linked cohort had preserved MMSE but was not uniformly CDR 0.
\item PET amyloid burden showed exploratory cortical-thickness association in sensorimotor regions.
\item PET-defined resting-state motor connectivity effects were weak after FDR correction.
\item Findings motivate age/sex-adjusted amyloid/tau PET studies with dexterity task-fMRI.
\end{itemize}
