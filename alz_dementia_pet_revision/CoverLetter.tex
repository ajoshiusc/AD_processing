Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript entitled ``Motor-system MRI correlates of amyloid PET burden in adults with preserved MMSE: an OASIS-3 study'' for publication in \emph{Alzheimer's \& Dementia}.

This study uses OASIS-3 amyloid PET as the primary biomarker variable, applying tracer-specific PET Unified Pipeline cutoffs for PiB and AV45. The manuscript evaluates PET-defined amyloid analyses as the primary biomarker framework and includes MMSE-based functional analyses only as exploratory cognitive-severity context.

The paper reports an exploratory association between amyloid PET burden and cortical thickness in sensorimotor and motor-adjacent regions among adults with preserved MMSE. We also report important cohort context, including CDR distribution and age/sex imbalance, and present PET-defined resting-state motor-connectivity effects transparently as weak and non-significant after FDR correction. These results motivate future amyloid/tau PET studies with covariate-adjusted structural analysis and task-fMRI studies of manual dexterity.

The manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration elsewhere. All authors have approved the submitted version and declare no conflicts of interest relevant to this work.

Sincerely,
